KEY FEATURES DEMONSTRATED:
================================================================================

1. AUTONOMOUS DETECTION (Detectors Panel)
   - 6 forensic detectors running in parallel
   - Status indicators: ✓ (complete) ● (running) ○ (queued)
   - Shows breadth of analysis without manual intervention

2. PRIORITIZED FINDINGS (Findings Panel)
   - Table sorted by severity (CRIT > HIGH > MED > LOW)
   - Cursor navigation with arrow keys
   - Analyst-actionable results with context

3. SELF-CORRECTION (Self-Correction Log)
   - Real-time contradiction detection and resolution
   - Counter showing 3 resolved contradictions
   - THE DIFFERENTIATOR: Visible autonomous healing

4. FORENSIC REASONING (Reasoning Panel)
   - Shows WHY, not just WHAT
   - Multi-artifact correlation (MFT SI vs FN)
   - Demonstrates forensic rigor and decision transparency
